# Community code of conduct / 社区行为规范

Be respectful, discuss technical evidence, and welcome people with different experience levels. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted contact and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

尊重不同经验的参与者，围绕技术证据讨论。不得骚扰、威胁、歧视、持续进行对方不接受的联系，或泄露个人信息。

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation, proportionate to the behavior. To report an incident, request a private channel from the maintainer without posting personal details publicly; a verified private contact must be configured before community launch. Reports are handled with discretion, and retaliation is unacceptable.

维护者可按行为严重程度移除有害内容或限制参与。报告事件时先请求私密渠道，不在公开讨论中披露个人细节；社区开放前应配置可用的私密联系方式。保护报告隐私，不允许报复。
